Features that
actually matter.

CharaROM improves performance, stability, and security over stock Android while bringing modern features. The difference? We have personality.

01 · Security & Privacy

Protection without compromise.

  • GrapheneOS and RestlessOS security and privacy enhancements
  • Duress PIN/Password (reimplemented from GrapheneOS)
  • Autoreboot after configurable idle period (from CalyxOS)
  • Systemwide block on reading adb/Developer Options status
  • Built-in Play Integrity Fix and TrickyStore
  • Sensor block per-package
  • Pocket detection prevents interaction when in pocket
  • Mic, camera, location, and media projection privacy indicators

02 · Built-in Apps

Useful stuff, no bloat.

  • microG-focused environment, promoting FOSS over Proprietary Software
  • AntiSafetyCore blocks harmful Google services
  • Vanadium and AOSmium recognized as system webviews
  • LibreDNS as a supported Private DNS option
  • No Face Unlock, AudioFX, MatLog, or LMODroid Freeform Sidebar
  • F-Droid Repo Ultralist, having multiple useful F-Droid repos from the start
  • Minimal connection to Google
